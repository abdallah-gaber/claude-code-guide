## The one idea that matters most

Everything else in this guide is detail. This is the thesis:

<CompareCard
  leftTitle="Vibe coding"
  leftTone="bad"
  leftPoints={[
    'Go with the flow, no code-specific instructions',
    'No real code review of what gets generated',
    'Fast to start, expensive to maintain',
  ]}
  rightTitle="Agentic engineering (this guide)"
  rightTone="good"
  rightPoints={[
    'You stay in control — you steer the AI',
    'You give specific instructions: libraries, patterns, conventions',
    'Code review is part of the loop, not an afterthought',

Claude Code is a very capable assistant. It is not a replacement for engineering judgment. Every
section in this guide assumes you're the one steering.

## Why this guide exists

I lead a Flutter team at [LinkDev](https://abdallahgaber.dev). This guide's first seed came from
a Claude Code course that my teammate [Ismail Elmogy](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ismail-elmogy/)
took and generously shared with the rest of us — the notes were good, but scattered across slide
decks and written for a general audience rather than for a team shipping real client apps under
real deadlines. This guide is what came out of turning that material into something we actually
use day to day: fewer slides, more "here's exactly what to type and why."

It's public and free. If you're not on my team and you find it useful anyway — welcome, that's
the point. Consider it a small giving-back project from LinkDev to the wider dev community.
